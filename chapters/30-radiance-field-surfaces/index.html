Sections

  1. 30.1 Implicit Surfaces and the Eikonal Equation Reviewed

    Topics: zero level set과 regular value, \(\mathbf{N} = \grad f/\abs{\grad f}\)(부호까지 정해짐), SDF와 medial axis, eikonal equation의 해는 많고 distance function은 viscosity solution, eikonal loss가 보장하지 않는 것, VolSDF·NeuS의 SDF→density와 weight의 bias, TSDF, SDF와 geodesic distance

  2. 30.2 Curvature of Implicit Surfaces Reviewed

    Topics: \(W_p = -\tfrac{1}{\abs{\grad f}}P\,\Hess f\), SDF의 Hessian eigenvalue = \(0, -\kappa_1, -\kappa_2\)와 offset, \(\divg\mathbf{N} = -2H\)와 \(\Delta f = -2H\), bordered Hessian의 \(K\), Neuralangelo curvature loss(L1, \(H = 0\)이지 평평함이 아님), marching cubes와 모호성 · Running examples: sphere, cylinder, torus of revolution, saddle

  3. 30.3 Surfels and 2D Gaussian Splatting Reviewed

    Topics: 2D Gaussian = 평면의 국소 parametrization(\(E = s_u^2\), \(G = s_v^2\), normal \(t_u \times t_v\)), ray–splat intersection = homography(두 직선의 cross product), edge-on과 low-pass filter, depth distortion = weight distribution의 분산, normal consistency = splat이 rendering된 surface에 접함, SuGaR와 Poisson, orientation

  4. 30.4 Depth Maps as Parametrized Surfaces Reviewed

    Topics: \(X = d\,\mathsf{K}^{-1}(u, v, 1)^{\mathsf T}\)는 depth가 smooth한 곳에서 언제나 regular, silhouette = 투영의 fold, footprint \(d^2\cos\alpha/(f_xf_y\abs{\cos\theta})\), grazing에서 불안정한 것과 아닌 것, 평면 ⇔ inverse depth가 affine, second fundamental form = \(-d\inner{\mathbf{N}}{X}\Hess\rho\), smoothness prior가 공짜로 두는 모양, depth Laplacian ≠ mean curvature
