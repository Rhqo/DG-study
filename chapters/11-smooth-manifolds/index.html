Sections

  1. 11.1 Topological Manifolds Reviewed

    Topics: locally Euclidean, topological manifold(Hausdorff·second countable), open subset과 곱, 예(\(\R^n\), 그래프, \(S^n\), \(\RP^n\), torus \(T^n\)), 비예(원점이 두 개인 직선, \(\R \times \R_{\text{discrete}}\), 십자), topological invariance of dimension(진술), chart와 coordinate functions, coordinate ball의 countable basis

  2. 11.2 Charts, Atlases, and Smooth Structures Reviewed

    Topics: transition map과 smooth compatibility, smooth atlas, maximal atlas와 smooth structure, atlas가 정하는 구조의 존재·유일성, \(\R^n\)의 표준 구조와 polar coordinates chart, \(\R\) 위의 서로 다른 구조(\(x^3\) chart), chart의 제한과 조정

  3. 11.3 Spheres and Stereographic Projection Reviewed

    Topics: stereographic projection의 transition map \(u \mapsto u/\abs{u}^2\)와 그 Jacobian matrix, hemisphere charts, 두 atlas가 같은 구조를 줌, \(S^1\)의 각 chart, chart 하나로는 compact manifold를 덮을 수 없음 · Running examples: sphere(\(S^n\)의 smooth structure와 stereographic projection)

  4. 11.4 More Examples Reviewed

    Topics: \(\RP^n\)의 transition map, open submanifold, vector space와 \(\GL(n,\R)\), 그래프, product manifold(곱 atlas는 극대가 아님) · Running examples: \(\RP^n\)(chart와 smooth structure); torus(\(T^2 = S^1 \times S^1\), 각 chart와 torus of revolution parametrization)

  5. 11.5 Manifolds with Boundary Reviewed

    Topics: closed upper half-space \(\mathbb{H}^n\), interior chart와 boundary chart, interior point와 boundary point, topological invariance of the boundary(진술), 임의의 부분집합 위의 smoothness, smooth invariance of the boundary(inverse function theorem으로 증명), \(\partial M\)의 smooth structure, closed ball \(\partial\overline{B}{}^n = S^{n-1}\)

  6. 11.6 Relation to Part I Planned

    Topics: parametrization과 chart의 관계(Chapter 6이 끝난 뒤 작성) · compatibility proposition: regular surface는 2-dimensional smooth manifold이고, parametrization의 inverse map은 smooth chart다
