40. Discrete Curvature I: The Integral Viewpoint

Track 2. Discrete Differential Geometry · Lectures: [CMU-DDG L16] (video, slides) · References: [Cr §5.5]

Threads: T1 What is curvature? (angle defect, curvature normals, and the polyhedral Steiner formula: smooth curvature integrated over dual cells) · T5 Integration and topology (the discrete Gauss–Bonnet theorem, exact on every mesh)

Questions

  • smooth curvature를 polyhedral surface의 vertex dual cell 위에서 적분하면 어떤 discrete curvature가 나오고, 그중 무엇이 정확히 보존되는가(Gauss–Bonnet)?
  • 전혀 다른 출발점인 Steiner formula(offset의 넓이와 부피)에서도 같은 discrete curvature가 다시 나오는가?
