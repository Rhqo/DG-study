Sections

  1. 12.1 Smooth Functions and Smooth Maps Reviewed

    Topics: coordinate representation과 chart에 무관함, \(C^\infty(M)\), smooth map의 기본 성질(continuity, locality, composition), 곱과 sphere로 들어가는 map, projection \(S^n \to \RP^n\), \(\GL(n, \R)\)의 곱과 inverse matrix, manifold with boundary의 경우 · compatibility proposition: regular surface 위 함수·map의 smoothness(Chapter 6)는 manifold로서의 smoothness와 같다(Proposition 12.1.11) · Running examples: sphere(height function의 stereographic projection에 대한 coordinate representation), \(\RP^n\)

  2. 12.2 Diffeomorphisms Reviewed

    Topics: diffeomorphism(Chapter 2와 6의 정의의 일반화), chart·boundary·dimension의 보존, \((\R, \mathcal{A}_3) \cong (\R, \mathcal{A}_1)\), non-example \(x \mapsto x^3\), local diffeomorphism · Running examples: torus \(T^2 \cong\) torus of revolution \(T_{R,r}\), \(\pi_S\colon S^n \to \RP^n\)은 local diffeomorphism

  3. 12.3 Bump Functions Reviewed

    Topics: \(e^{-1/t}\)로 만든 smooth cutoff function, support, \(\R^n\)과 manifold 위의 bump function, compact set의 bump function, 국소적으로 정의된 함수의 extension, \(C^\infty(M)\)은 infinite-dimensional

  4. 12.4 Exhaustion by Compact Sets and Partitions of Unity Reviewed

    Topics: local finiteness, partition of unity, local compactness와 exhaustion by compact sets(second countability 사용), partition of unity의 existence theorem, paracompactness(Remark)

  5. 12.5 Applications of Partitions of Unity Reviewed

    Topics: closed set의 bump function(smooth Urysohn lemma), extension lemma, closed regular surface 위의 함수를 \(\R^3\) 전체로 확장하기, exhaustion function · Running examples: torus of revolution 위의 \(\cos u\)의 extension
